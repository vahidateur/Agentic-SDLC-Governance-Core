# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is committed to a professional, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in its repositories, discussions, reviews, events, and governance processes. Contributions are evaluated on substance, evidence, and alignment with project standards—not identity, background, seniority, or affiliation.

Participants are expected to act in good faith, communicate clearly, assume constructive intent while addressing impact, and protect confidential information. Disagreement is welcome when reasoned, relevant, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming, inclusive, and professional language.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, cultures, and communication styles.
- Give and receive actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Explain decisions, constraints, and evidence transparently.
- Respect project scope, review processes, traceability, provenance, and Human approval gates.
- Credit others' work and preserve context needed for reliable attribution.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and personal information.
- Escalate concerns through the appropriate reporting process instead of personal attacks or public speculation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwanted contact, or sexualized content.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, baiting, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information or confidential material without authorization.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, security reports, or exploitable vulnerability details publicly before review.
- Impersonation, fraud, plagiarism, misrepresentation of approvals, or falsification of evidence or provenance.
- Retaliation against anyone raising a concern or participating in an investigation.
- Sustained off-topic promotion, spam, or conduct materially disrupting project work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers clarify and enforce this standard. They may document incidents, request corrective behavior, remove or edit contributions, close discussions, restrict participation temporarily or permanently, or escalate matters to the relevant organizational process. Responses are proportionate to severity, frequency, intent, and impact and may occur without public disclosure of private details.

Maintainers will seek to handle reports fairly, preserve relevant evidence, limit access to sensitive information, and avoid conflicts of interest where practical. Enforcement decisions must not be represented as approvals for unrelated governance changes.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through the repository's configured moderation or support channel. If the concern involves a security vulnerability, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not disclose it publicly before review.

A useful report includes what happened, when and where it occurred, people or project spaces involved, relevant links or Evidence Artifact references, and immediate safety or confidentiality concerns. Redact secrets and personal information where possible. Do not retaliate, investigate beyond what is necessary to preserve information, or publish allegations while a private review is in progress.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions when an individual represents or materially participates in the project.
